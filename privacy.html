LineMarks Privacy Policy

Effective: November 27, 2026

LineMarks ("the extension") is a browser extension that saves the exact line where you stopped reading on a webpage and scrolls you back to it on revisit.

Data we handle

When you save a line, the extension stores on your own device, in your browser's built-in extension storage: the page URL, page title, time of saving, the selected text quote, highlight color, and any note or tags you type. This data never leaves your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no remote server, and no data sale or transfer to any third party.

Permissions and why

  • storage — keeps your bookmarks and preferences on-device.
  • activeTab — saves and restores highlights in the tab you invoked the extension on.
  • contextMenus — adds the "Bookmark this line" right-click entry.
  • scripting — loads the highlighter on tabs already open at install time.
  • alarms — a once-daily housekeeping tick (service workers cannot use timers).
  • host access (http/https) — lets saved lines be highlighted on the pages you saved them on.

Your control

You can view, edit, export, or permanently delete your data at any time from the extension's Library dashboard or popup. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
